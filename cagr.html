CAGR Calculator (Compound Annual Growth Rate)

Measure the constant annualized growth rate of your investment portfolio over multi-year periods, smoothing out market volatility.

CAGR Mathematical Formula

CAGR = (Ending Value / Beginning Value)^(1 / Years) – 1

Calculate CAGR Now

Why CAGR is Superior to Absolute Returns

Absolute returns show overall gain but ignore the time factor. CAGR accounts for the geometric compounding effect, allowing you to accurately compare a stock's performance against mutual funds, gold, or real estate.
