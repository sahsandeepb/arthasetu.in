Kisko Overdraft Loan Lena Chahiye?

  • Take Overdraft If: Aap business owner ya consultant hain jinke paas saal bhar ₹5L to ₹15L ka working capital / tax cash account me pada rehta hai.
  • Stick to Regular Loan If: Aap salaried employee hain jinki salary aate hi EMIs, rent aur kharchon me khatam ho jaati hai aur account me sirf ₹20k–₹50k bachta hai.
