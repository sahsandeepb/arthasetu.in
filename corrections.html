Formula Accuracy & Corrections Policy

ArthaSetu India is committed to complete mathematical transparency. We actively encourage financial researchers, Chartered Accountants, and developers to audit our open engines.

Reporting an Algorithmic or Slab Discrepancy

If you identify a calculation edge-case, incorrect standard deduction threshold, or formula rounding bug, please submit a report with:

  • The specific calculator mode (e.g., Tax Breakeven, Home Loan Prepayment).
  • Input values used and expected vs. actual outputs.
  • Statutory reference or CBDT/RBI notification source.

📧 Direct Submissions: corrections@arthasetu.in

⏱️ SLA: Discrepancies are typically audited and resolved on production within 24 business hours.
