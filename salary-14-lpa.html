Tax Impact on a ₹14 Lakh Package

On a ₹14,00,000 CTC, after deducting employer EPF contributions and applying the ₹75,000 Standard Deduction, your net taxable salary stands at approximately ₹12,57,000. Under Section 115BAC, income between ₹12 Lakhs and ₹16 Lakhs attracts a 15% slab rate. Because the lower slabs enjoy low rates, your total tax remains substantially lower than Old Regime tax unless extensive deductions are claimed.

The 14 LPA Breakeven Rule

To make the Old Tax Regime more beneficial than the New Tax Regime on a ₹14 Lakh package, an individual must claim eligible tax deductions (Section 80C + 80D + HRA rent exemption + Home Loan interest + NPS) totaling over ₹3,50,000. If your total deductible expenses fall below this threshold, the New Tax Regime provides superior take-home pay with zero investment lock-in.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the exact monthly take-home for 14 LPA under the New Tax Regime?

Assuming 40% basic salary and standard 12% employee EPF, your monthly in-hand take-home salary is approximately ₹1,01,000 per month.

Can employees opt for statutory ₹1,800 EPF capping on 14 LPA?

Yes, depending on employer HR policy. Opting for the ₹1,800/month EPF cap instead of 12% of actual basic can increase your monthly cash take-home by over ₹3,800 per month on a 14 LPA package.

Are tax surcharges applicable on a 14 LPA salary?

No. Income tax surcharge in India applies only when total net taxable income exceeds ₹50,00,000.
