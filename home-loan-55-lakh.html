Why Prepaying a ₹55 Lakh Home Loan Saves Over ₹17 Lakhs

On a ₹55,00,000 home loan at 8.5% over 20 years, your total interest payout alone comes to over ₹59.55 Lakhs—exceeding the original principal borrowed. By prepaying just ₹5,500 each month directly toward principal, you eliminate nearly 5 years of compounding interest cycles from the tail end of your amortization schedule.

Are there prepayment charges on a ₹55 Lakh housing loan?

No. Under RBI directions, banks and HFCs cannot levy prepayment or foreclosure charges on individual floating-rate housing loans.

Should I reduce EMI or reduce tenure?

Keeping your EMI unchanged and reducing tenure maximizes interest savings. Reducing EMI lowers monthly cash outflow but keeps interest compounding across the full 20 years.
