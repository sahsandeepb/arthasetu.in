Tax Impact on a ₹20 Lakh Package

On a ₹20,00,000 CTC, after removing standard employer EPF contributions and applying the ₹75,000 Standard Deduction, your net taxable salary is around ₹18,29,000. Under Section 115BAC, income above ₹16 Lakhs attracts a 20% slab rate. Because the New Tax Regime slabs are wide and progressive, your effective income tax stays substantially lower than Old Regime tax unless massive exemptions are claimed.

The 20 LPA Breakeven Rule

To make the Old Tax Regime more financially attractive on a ₹20 Lakh salary, an individual must claim eligible tax deductions (Section 80C + 80D + HRA rent exemption + NPS) totaling over ₹4,25,000. If your total deductible expenses fall below this threshold, the New Tax Regime provides superior take-home pay with zero investment lock-in.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the exact monthly take-home for 20 LPA under the New Tax Regime?

Assuming 40% basic salary and standard 12% employee EPF, your monthly in-hand take-home salary is approximately ₹1,36,000 per month.

Is EPF capped at ₹1,800 or deducted at 12% of basic?

By default, private corporate employers deduct 12% of actual basic pay. However, some employers provide an option to cap PF contribution at the statutory ₹1,800/month (12% of ₹15,000 ceiling), which can increase your monthly cash-in-hand.

Are tax surcharges applicable on a 20 LPA salary?

No. Income tax surcharge in India applies only when total net taxable income exceeds ₹50,00,000.
