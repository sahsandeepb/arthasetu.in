FD Maturity Compounding Formula

A = P × (1 + r / n)^(n × t)

  • A: Maturity Payout Amount
  • P: Principal Deposited
  • r: Annual Interest Rate (Decimal)
  • n: Compounding frequency per year (n = 4 for Indian scheduled banks)
  • t: Deposit tenure in years

Taxation on Bank Fixed Deposits

Interest earned on Fixed Deposits is fully taxable according to your income tax slab under "Income from Other Sources".

  • TDS on FD: Banks deduct TDS at 10% under Section 194A if total FD interest exceeds ₹40,000 in a financial year (₹50,000 for senior citizens).
  • Form 15G / 15H: Can be submitted if total annual income is below the taxable threshold to avoid TDS deduction.
