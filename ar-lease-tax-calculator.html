How Corporate Car Leasing Creates Massive Tax Savings

When you buy a car through a personal bank loan, your EMI, fuel, maintenance, and insurance are paid from post-tax net salary on which you have already paid 30% tax. Under an employer corporate lease, the leasing rental and operational expenses are deducted directly from your pre-tax gross salary before tax is calculated.

Rule 3 Statutory Perquisite Valuation

Because the employee uses the company-provided car for personal travel, the Income Tax Department adds a small, fixed perquisite value to the employee's taxable income under Rule 3:

  • Engine capacity ≤ 1600cc: Taxable perquisite is fixed at ₹1,800 per month (₹21,600/year).
  • Engine capacity > 1600cc: Taxable perquisite is fixed at ₹2,400 per month (₹28,800/year).
  • Chauffeur / Driver: If the employer reimburses driver salary, an additional flat ₹900 per month (₹10,800/year) is added.

Frequently Asked Questions

What happens to the car at the end of the lease tenure?

At the end of the 3, 4, or 5-year lease term, the employee usually has two choices: (1) purchase the car at the residual market value (usually 15% to 20% of original price), or (2) surrender the car to the leasing company and start a fresh lease for a new vehicle.

What if I switch companies while the car lease is active?

If you change employers, you can either: (1) transfer the existing lease to your new employer if they have an active corporate car lease policy with the same leasing partner, or (2) foreclose the lease by paying the outstanding principal balance and applicable taxes.

Does corporate car lease work under the New Tax Regime?

Under Section 115BAC (New Tax Regime), most corporate salary perks and non-monetary exemptions are disallowed. Employees choosing corporate car lease typically benefit far more under the Old Tax Regime.
