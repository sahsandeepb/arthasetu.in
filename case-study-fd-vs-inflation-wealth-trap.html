THE FIXED DEPOSIT ILLUSION Paper Profit vs Real Purchasing Loss ₹10 Lakh FD 15 saal baad ₹27 Lakh dikhegi, par khareedne ki taakat sirf ₹8.5 Lakh bachegi!

Bank me jaakar Fixed Deposit (FD) karwana har Indian parivaar ka sabse safe option maana jata hai. Bank receipt par likha hota hai: "7.1% Guaranteed Return". Lekin agar aap 20% ya 30% income tax slab me aate hain, toh tax katne aur 6% mehangai ke baad aapka paisa badh nahi raha, balki har saal quietly deplete ho raha hai.

The Silent Wealth Tax: Agar aapko FD par 7% byaaj milta hai aur aap 30% tax bracket me hain, toh TDS aur income tax ke baad aapka net in-hand return sirf 4.8% reh jata hai. Agar market me doodh, school fees, aur medical kharche har saal 6% badh rahe hain, toh aap har saal -1.2% ke negative return par chal rahe hain!

Kyu Bank FD Mehangai Ko Beat Nahi Kar Sakti?

Bank FD ek fixed debt instrument hai. Isme do sabse badi kamiyan hain:

  • No Indexation / Slab Taxation: FD ke byaaj par koi concessional capital gains tax nahi milta. Har saal accrual basis par aapke highest slab rate par tax lagta hai.
  • Purchasing Power Erosion: 15 saal baad bank passbook me ₹10 Lakh badhkar ₹20 Lakh ya ₹25 Lakh toh dikhenge, lekin jo car ya flat aaj ₹10 Lakh me aata hai, 15 saal baad wahi cheez lene ke liye ₹24 Lakh chahiye honge.
  • Solution (Smart Asset Allocation): Emergency funds aur 1-2 saal ki zaroorat ke liye FD theek hai, lekin 5 saal se lambe goals ke liye Index Funds ya Hybrid Funds hi inflation ko beat kar sakte hain.
