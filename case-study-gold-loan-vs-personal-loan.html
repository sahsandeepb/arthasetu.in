Emergency me log aksar sochte hain: "Ghar me sona pada hai, 9% par Gold Loan le lete hain; Personal Loan par 12.5% byaaj kyu bharein?" Lekin dono loans ka repayment structure zameen-aasmaan jaisa alag hota hai!

The Bullet Repayment Trap: Gold loan me monthly EMI nahi hoti (Bullet repayment). Iska matlab har mahine aapka principal wahi ka wahi rehta hai aur pure principal par interest compound hota rehta hai. Personal loan me har mahine principal kam hota hai, jisse aakhiri me byaaj kam banta hai!

Kaunsa Loan Kab Lena Chahiye?

  • Gold Loan (Best for 3 to 6 Months): Agar aapko pata hai ki 2-3 mahine me lump sum paisa aane wala hai aur aap loan close kar denge, toh Gold Loan sabse sasta padta hai.
  • Personal Loan (Best for 1 to 3 Years): Agar monthly salary se thoda-thoda karke chukana hai, toh reducing-balance Personal Loan safer hai; koi sona girvi rakhne ka darr nahi hota.
