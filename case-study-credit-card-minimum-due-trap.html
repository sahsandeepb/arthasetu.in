The 42% APR Revolving Trap

Credit Card Ka 'Minimum Due' Bharne Ki Galti? ₹1 Lakh Ka Bill 15 Saal Me ₹3.8 Lakh Kaise Banta Hai Dekhein

Updated: September 2026 • Reading Time: 3 Mins • Banking Research Desk
THE MINIMUM AMOUNT DUE ILLUSION Borrowed ₹1 Lakh • Repaid ₹3.84 Lakhs Kyu sirf 5% Minimum Due pay karne par bank aapse 14 saal tak byaaj vasool karta hai!

Credit Card statement aate hi sabse upar bold text me do numbers dikhte hain: "Total Amount Due: ₹1,00,000" aur "Minimum Amount Due: ₹5,000". 90% log rahat ki saans lete hue sirf ₹5,000 pay kar dete hain yeh soch kar ki *"Chalo, late payment charge se bach gaye aur CIBIL score bhi safe hai."* Lekin yeh bank ka sabse bada compounding trap hota hai!

The Hidden Interest Trap: Minimum Due bharne par aap late fee se toh bach jaate hain, lekin aapka **45-din ka Interest-Free Grace Period turant cancel ho jata hai**. Bank bache hue balance par 3.5% per month (42% per year) ka compounding byaaj lagata hai, aur sath hi har byaaj ke upar **18% GST** alag se judta hai!

Kyu Bank Chahta Hai Ki Aap Sirf Minimum Due Bharein?

Credit card companies ka 70% se zyada profit unhi logon se aata hai jo revolving credit me phas jaate hain:

  • Loss of Free Credit Period: Jab tak pichla balance 100% clear nahi hota, aap card se agla koi bhi transaction (jaise petrol ya grocery) karenge, us par khareedne ke pehle din se 42% byaaj lagna shuru ho jata hai.
  • The 18% GST Hammer: Agar bank aapse ₹4,000 byaaj leta hai, toh ₹720 government ka GST alag se katega. Yeh byaaj aur GST milkar principal ko kabhi khatam hi nahi hone dete.
  • The Golden Rule: Credit Card ka bill hamesha "Total Amount Due" par full pay karein. Agar paise kam hain, toh use turant bank ke low-interest EMI (14%–16%) me convert karwayein, par 42% revolving balance par kabhi mat chodiye!
