THE 84-MONTH LOW EMI ILLUSION Save ₹3,000 in Monthly EMI • Lose ₹2.2 Lakh in Extra Interest Kyu 7 saal ka car loan aapki pocket se ₹10 Lakh ki car par ₹14.5 Lakh vasool karta hai!

Showroom me car select karte hi salesperson smiling face ke sath bolta hai: "Sir, budget tight hai toh chinta mat kijiye. 5 saal ke bajay 7 saal (84 months) ka loan karwa dete hain. Monthly EMI ₹21,000 se ghat kar sirf ₹16,000 reh jayegi!" Lakho buyers bina calculator chalaye 7-year loan sign kar dete hain. Lekin kya low EMI ka matlab sasta loan hota hai?

The Dealership Trick: EMI ₹5,000 kam zarur lagti hai, lekin 24 extra mahine tak 9.5% byaaj lagte rehne se bank aapse **₹1,80,000 se ₹2,50,000 extra byaaj** vasool karta hai. Aur sabse bada khatra: 5 saal baad jab car ki market value sirf ₹4 Lakh bachti hai, tab bhi aapka bank balance loan ₹4.5 Lakh bacha rehta hai (Negative Equity Trap)!

Kyu 7-Year Loan Financial Disaster Hai?

Home loan 20 saal ka ho sakta hai kyunki property appreciate hoti hai. Lekin car har saal 15% depreciate hoti hai:

  • The Negative Equity Trap (Underwater Loan): 4th aur 5th saal me car par battery replacement, tyres, suspension aur engine maintenance ka kharcha shuru ho jata hai. Agar aap car bechna chahenge, toh market price loan chukane ke liye bhi poori nahi hogi.
  • Zero-Dep Insurance Ends: 5 saal baad bumper-to-bumper zero depreciation insurance nahi milta. Accident hone par claim ka 50% pocket se dena padta hai jabki EMI abhi bhi 2 saal bachi hoti hai.
  • The Golden 20/4/10 Rule: Car khareedte waqt minimum 20% down payment karein, loan tenure **maximum 4 saal** rakhein, aur monthly EMI aapki monthly take-home salary ke 10% se zyada nahi honi chahiye.
