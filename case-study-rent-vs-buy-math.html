Kyu Renter Jeet Jata Hai?

Real estate me 3 hidden costs hote hain jo flat buyer ko pata nahi chalte:

  • Heavy Interest Outflow: ₹80 Lakh ke loan par aap 20 saal me ₹86 Lakh interest bharte hain. Flat ki kimat badh kar ₹2.9 Crore hoti hai, lekin aapki pocket se ₹1.86 Crore ja chuke hote hain.
  • Rental Yield is Just 2.5% to 3%: India me residential properties ka rental yield sirf 2.5% se 3% hai. Matlab ₹1 Crore ka flat lene ke bajay aap use saal ke sirf ₹3 Lakh me rent par le sakte hain!
  • The Down Payment Compounding: ₹20 Lakh ka down payment jo buyer cement me lock karta hai, renter use equity fund me daalta hai, jo akele 12% return par 20 saal me ₹1.93 Crore ban jata hai!
