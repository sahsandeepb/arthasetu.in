Kyu Kaam Karta Hai Yeh 1 Extra EMI Formula?

Home loan reducing balance method par chalta hai. Har mahine bank aapke bache hue balance par byaaj lagata hai. Jab aap saal me 12 ke bajay 13 EMIs dete hain (ya bonus aane par ek EMI extra bharte hain), toh woh pura ka pura 13wa payment 100% principal se deduct hota hai.

Isse agle mahine ka balance gir jata hai aur aane wale saalon ka compounding cycle hamesha ke liye wipe out ho jata hai. RBI rules ke anusaar individual floating home loans par bank koi bhi prepayment penalty nahi laga sakta.
