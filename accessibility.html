Accessibility Statement

ArthaSetu India is committed to ensuring that our financial calculation workspace is accessible to all users, including individuals with disabilities.

Accessibility Standards Met:

  • WCAG 2.1 Level AA Compliance: High-contrast color ratios across light and dark modes.
  • Keyboard Navigation: Full focus state support for input fields, mode selectors, and actions.
  • Screen Reader Friendly: Semantic HTML5 form controls and clean ARIA live regions for calculation metrics.
  • Touch Target Sizing: All tap targets strictly meet the 44x44px mobile touch standards.
