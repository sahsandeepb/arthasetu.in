THE 30% UTILIZATION TRAP Zero Late Payments, Yet 40-Point Drop? Dekhein kaise card limit ka 70% use karna bina default ke CIBIL crash karta hai

Ek vyakti har mahine apne credit card ka bill aane ke agle din hi 100% full pay kar deta hai. Fir bhi jab woh CIBIL report check karta hai, toh score 785 se gir kar 732 ho jata hai! Bank me jao toh kehte hain: *"Sir, system-generated hai."* Lekin iske peeche ek simple mathematics hoti hai!

The Statement Date Trap: Bank aapka utilization tab report nahi karta jab aap bill bharte hain; bank report karta hai Statement Generation Date par. Agar aapki limit ₹1,00,000 hai aur bill banne ke din outstanding ₹75,000 tha, toh CIBIL ko report jata hai 75% CUR (Credit Hungry), jisse CIBIL algorithm score gira deta hai!
