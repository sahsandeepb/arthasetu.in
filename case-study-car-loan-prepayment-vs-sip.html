Kyu Car Loan Prepayment Aksar Galat Decision Hota Hai?

Home loan aur car loan ke mathematical nature me zameen-aasmaan ka fark hota hai:

  • Prepayment Charges (Foreclosure Penalty): Home loans par RBI floating rate loans par penalty ban karta hai, lekin fixed car loans par banks 3% se 5% foreclosure fees charge karte hain. ₹6 Lakh par ₹18,000 se ₹30,000 seedhe penalty me chale jaate hain.
  • The Arbitrage Spread (13% vs 9%): Agar aapka loan 9.2% par hai aur equity compounding 13% deti hai, toh 3.8% ka positive spread aapke corpus par saal dar saal multiply hota hai.
  • Emergency Liquidity Shield: Loan chukane ke baad aapka cash khatam ho jata hai. Agar aage chalkar medical ya job emergency aayi, toh naya personal loan 14%–16% par lena padta hai. Cash invest rakhne se liquidity hamesha bani rehti hai.
