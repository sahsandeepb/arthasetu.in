Editorial & Mathematical Verification Policy

Last Updated: 2026 | Version: 2.4-Production

ArthaSetu India follows rigorous editorial and mathematical integrity standards to provide Indian taxpayers, salaried employees, and retail investors with accurate, unbiased financial intelligence.

1. Primary Source Statutory Grounding

All formulas, tax slabs, and regulatory deductions are sourced directly from official statutory publications:

  • Income Tax & Direct Taxes: Central Board of Direct Taxes (CBDT), Ministry of Finance, and Union Budget Notifications.
  • Loan & EMI Models: Reserve Bank of India (RBI) circulars on reducing balance interest schedules and benchmark rates.
  • Indirect Taxes: GST Council of India statutory tariff schedules and GST portal guidelines.

2. Algorithmic Verification & Zero Bias

Our calculation engines are completely independent of commercial influence. No bank, NBFC, mutual fund distributor, or financial institution can pay to alter calculation outputs, ranking metrics, or regime comparison breakevens.

3. Regular Review & Budget Audits

The mathematical models are audited within 24 hours of any Union Budget, GST Council notification, or RBI monetary policy adjustment.
