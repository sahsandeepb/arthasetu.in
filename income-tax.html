Key Deductions & Exemptions Explained

  • Standard Deduction: ₹75,000 for salaried employees and pensioners under the New Regime (₹50,000 under Old Regime).
  • Section 87A Rebate: Up to ₹60,000 under the New Regime for taxable income ≤ ₹12,00,000.
  • Marginal Relief: Provided if taxable income marginally exceeds ₹12,00,000 so that tax payable does not exceed the excess income above ₹12 Lakhs.
  • Health & Education Cess: A mandatory 4% cess is levied on total tax liability and surcharge.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Can I switch between New and Old regimes every year?
Salaried individuals without business income can choose the more beneficial regime each financial year while filing their ITR.
