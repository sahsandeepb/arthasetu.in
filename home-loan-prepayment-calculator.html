Why Home Loan Prepayments Yield Massive Returns

In a standard 20-year home loan at 8.5% interest, your total interest liability often equals or exceeds the borrowed principal amount. Because Indian banks calculate interest on a monthly reducing balance, every additional rupee paid above your standard EMI directly strikes off principal debt. This permanently wipes out compounding interest from the final years of your loan schedule.

The 1 Extra EMI Strategy

Financial planners recommend paying just 1 extra EMI per year (or increasing your monthly EMI by roughly 8% to 10%). On a ₹40 Lakh home loan for 20 years, this single habit can save more than ₹12 Lakhs in interest and eliminate over 4.5 years of debt.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my bank charge a fee if I prepay my home loan?

No. Under RBI directions, banks, Housing Finance Companies (HFCs), and NBFCs cannot levy any prepayment penalty or foreclosure charges on floating-rate individual home loans.

Should I prepay early in the loan tenure or later?

Prepayments made during the first 5 to 7 years save the most money. In the initial years, up to 70% of your EMI goes purely toward interest, making early principal cuts far more impactful.

Does prepayment reduce tax benefits under Section 24(b)?

Yes, reducing total interest paid will eventually reduce your annual interest deduction under Section 24(b) (capped at ₹2,00,000/year). However, the actual interest money saved from your pocket is far greater than the tax rebate.
