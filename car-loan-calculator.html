How Car Loan EMIs are Computed in India

A car loan uses the standard reducing-balance amortization model. Every monthly installment includes a principal component and an interest component. In the initial years, a higher proportion of the EMI goes toward interest, gradually shifting toward principal repayment as the outstanding debt diminishes.

The 20/4/10 Rule for Car Purchases

To keep auto financing financially sound, personal finance experts advise following the 20/4/10 guideline:

  • Make at least a 20% down payment on the on-road price.
  • Limit your loan tenure to no more than 4 years (48 months).
  • Keep total vehicle operating expenses (EMI + fuel + insurance) under 10% of your gross monthly income.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is zero down payment advisable for a car purchase?

100% on-road financing increases your monthly EMI and overall interest burden. Because vehicles depreciate rapidly (typically 15% to 20% in the first year), zero down payment can put you in a negative equity position where you owe more than the car's resale value.

Fixed vs Floating Rate: Which is better for a vehicle loan?

Most Indian banks offer fixed-rate car loans where installments remain constant throughout the tenure. Floating-rate loans are linked to the repo rate (EBLR/RLLR), meaning installments can fluctuate with RBI monetary policy changes.

Can I prepay my car loan early to save interest?

Yes. Prepaying extra sums toward principal directly reduces your outstanding balance and overall interest outgo. Check your lender's lock-in period and foreclosure terms before making lump-sum payments.
