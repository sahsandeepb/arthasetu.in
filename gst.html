GST Formula Cheat Sheet

1. Adding GST (Exclusive): GST Amount = (Base Price × GST Rate) / 100

2. Extracting GST (Inclusive): Base Price = Inclusive Amount / (1 + (GST Rate / 100))

GST Tax Brackets in India

  • 5%: Household essentials, packaged food items, and basic medicines.
  • 12%: Processed foods, business services, and computers.
  • 18%: Most standard consumer goods, software, dining, and professional services.
  • 28%: Luxury automobiles, aerated beverages, and demerit goods.
