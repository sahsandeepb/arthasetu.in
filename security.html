Security Architecture & Client-Side Sandbox

ArthaSetu India was architected from day one as a zero-trust, client-side only calculation engine.

1. Client-Side Execution Sandbox

Every salary number, loan principal, and tax deduction you enter remains isolated in your device's memory. No data is sent over the wire to backend databases.

2. Strict Transport Security (HSTS) & HTTPS

All traffic is strictly enforced over TLS 1.3 encryption with modern HTTP security headers.

3. Third-Party Dependency Isolation

We do not load external tracker libraries or heavy analytics bundles. Our engine is built with clean Vanilla JavaScript, minimizing exposure to third-party script vulnerabilities.
