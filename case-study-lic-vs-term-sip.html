The Golden Rule of Personal Finance:

  • Insurance is for Protection, Not Investment: Jeevan bima parivaar ki security ke liye hota hai, munafa kamane ke liye nahi. Bima hamesha **Pure Term Insurance** se karein jisme kam paise me ₹1 Crore se ₹2 Crore ka cover milta hai.
  • Invest the Rest in Equity: Bacha hua sara premium Nifty 50 Index Fund ya Flexicap Fund me daalein. 20 saal me compounding aapko financial independence dila degi!
