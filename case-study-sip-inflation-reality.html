Kaise Jeetein Mehangai Se? "10% Step-Up Rule"

Agar aap chahte hain ki 20 saal baad aapke paas asli wealth bache, toh flat SIP mat karein. Har saal appraisal ke waqt apni SIP ko sirf 10% badha dijiye (jaise pehle saal ₹5,000, doosre saal ₹5,500, teesre saal ₹6,050).

  • Normal Flat SIP: 20 saal me aap ₹12 Lakh invest karte hain aur ₹1 Crore banta hai (Asli purchasing power: ~₹31 Lakhs).
  • 10% Step-Up SIP: 20 saal me aap lagbhag ₹34 Lakh invest karte hain, lekin aapka final corpus badhkar ₹2.4 Crore se zyada ho jata hai!
  • Isse mehangai cut hone ke baad bhi aapke paas aaj ke ₹75 Lakh se zyada ki asli purchasing value bachti hai.
